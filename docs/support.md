# Support

Jogger is built and supported by [mundo.dev](https://mundo.dev).

## Getting help

Email **support@mundo.dev** and include:

- your store's `.myshopify.com` address
- the theme version, from **Theme settings → the panel at the top**
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- a screenshot, if it is something you can see

Most questions are answered within one working day.

## Before you write

A good half of what reaches us is already covered:

- **[Troubleshooting & FAQ](04-faq.html)** — the common ones
- **[Setting up](02-setup.html)** — menus, logo, home page, product pages
- **[Customizing the look](03-customizing.html)** — colours, type, layout

## What support covers

Questions about using Jogger, and anything in the theme that does not behave the
way this documentation says it should.

## What it does not

Changes to your store's own code, third-party apps, and custom development. If
you need the theme changed for your store specifically, say so in your email and
we can talk about it separately.
